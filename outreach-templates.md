# Avera Outreach Templates

> Created: 2026-03-03
> Use these as starting points. Personalize each message with the prospect's company, recent news, or specific M&A angle.

---

## 1. Cold LinkedIn Connection Request (300 char limit)

### Template A — Direct
```
Hi [First Name], I run an M&A advisory practice for growth-stage companies — your space caught my eye. Would love to connect and share a few thoughts on how acquisitions could accelerate [Company]'s roadmap. No pitch, just a conversation.
```

### Template B — Mutual Context
```
Hi [First Name], I led corp dev at Wellhub and Zendesk (closed $1B+ in deals) and now help growth-stage founders think through acquisitions. [Company]'s trajectory looks like a great fit for inorganic growth. Would love to connect.
```

---

## 2. Cold LinkedIn Follow-Up Message (after connection accepted)

### Template A — Credential-Led
```
Thanks for connecting, [First Name].

Quick context — I spent 10 years doing M&A at companies like Zendesk and Wellhub, where I led acquisitions that drove major product and market expansion. I started Avera to bring that same level of deal execution to founders who want to grow through acquisitions but don't have a dedicated corp dev team yet.

I've been following [Company] and think there's a compelling M&A angle — especially around [specific angle: e.g., "acquiring AI capabilities to strengthen your platform" / "consolidating smaller players in your space" / "expanding into adjacent verticals"].

Would you be open to a 20-minute call this week? Happy to share what I'm seeing in the market — no strings attached.
```

### Template B — Problem-Led
```
Thanks for connecting, [First Name].

I work with growth-stage founders who know they should be looking at acquisitions but don't have the bandwidth or in-house team to actually execute. I step in as your deal lead and own the process end to end — sourcing, diligence, negotiation, close.

Before starting Avera, I ran corp dev at Wellhub and Zendesk and closed $1B+ in deals. Happy to share what I'm seeing in [their sector] if you're curious — no agenda.

Would a quick call this week work?
```

## 3. Warm Intro Request (to send to your mutual connection)

```
Hey [Connector's Name],

I'm reaching out to a few founders in [sector] who might benefit from M&A advisory help. I noticed you're connected to [Founder Name] at [Company].

Quick context: I started Avera after running corp dev at Wellhub and Zendesk (closed $1B+ in deals). I help growth-stage companies execute acquisitions and strategic partnerships without needing to build an internal team.

[Company] looks like they could be in a great position to grow through acquisitions — [1 sentence on why: e.g., "especially with their recent Series C and push into new markets"].

Would you be comfortable making an intro? Happy to send you a forwardable blurb if that's easier.

Thanks!
Ilene
```

### Forwardable Blurb (for the connector to forward)
```
Hi [Founder Name],

I wanted to introduce you to Ilene Chen. She runs Avera, an M&A advisory practice for growth-stage companies. Before that, she led corp dev at Wellhub and Zendesk, closing $1B+ in deals.

She helps founders who want to grow through acquisitions but don't have a dedicated deal team yet. Given where [Company] is right now, I thought it could be a worthwhile conversation.

I'll let you two take it from here!
```

## Usage Notes

- **Personalize the M&A angle** for each prospect using the "Why M&A fits" and "Outreach hook" fields in prospect-tracker.md
- **Lead with credential, not the ask** — let the track record do the selling
- **Keep it short** — founders skim. Front-load the value
- **Don't say "fractional"** — position as "dedicated deal lead" or "on-demand corp dev"
- **AI angle:** Use in outreach hooks where relevant (e.g., "acquiring AI capabilities") but don't lead with it publicly
