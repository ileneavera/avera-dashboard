# Avera — M&A Superagent

## What This Product Does

Avera is an AI-powered command center for M&A and partnerships advisory. It helps dealmakers manage their entire workflow in one place:

- **Deal Pipeline**: Track active deals (company, sector, deal size, stage, diligence progress)
- **Outreach**: Automated campaign management for sourcing — send personalized outreach, track responses and meetings
- **Research**: AI-curated M&A market trends, sector sentiment (hot/moderate/cooling), weekly intelligence briefs
- **Due Diligence**: Pre-LOI and Post-LOI checklists covering financial, legal, and operational areas
- **Financial Modeling**: Upload financials (P&L, balance sheet, cash flow), generate projected models with revenue, EBITDA, FCF, and implied enterprise values
- **Risk Analysis**: Structured risk dashboard with severity ratings (high/medium/low), specific remediation steps, and pre-close vs post-close action items
- **Document Templates**: LOI, term sheet, NDA, APA, SPA, management services agreement

## Who It's For

- Ilene Chen (founder) running M&A and partnerships advisory
- Target users: independent sponsors, boutique M&A advisors, small PE firms, corp dev teams

## How It's Built

- Current state: single-page HTML/CSS mockup deployed on Surge (avera.surge.sh)
- Purple/pink accent color scheme, card-based UI, sidebar navigation
- No backend or database yet — this is the design prototype

## What We Don't Do

- Avera is not a CRM — it's deal-workflow-focused, not general sales
- Not a replacement for legal counsel — templates are starting points, not final docs
- Not a trading platform or public markets tool — focused on private M&A and partnerships
