# VC & Network Reactivation Messages

> Created: 2026-03-03
> Goal: Re-engage your existing network to generate warm intros to Series B-D founders who could use M&A advisory.

---

## 1. To VCs / Board Members You Know

### Template A — Direct Ask
```
Hi [Name],

Hope you're doing well. Quick update — I left Wellhub last year and started Avera, an M&A advisory practice for growth-stage companies. I work with founders as their dedicated deal lead, owning acquisitions from strategy to close.

I'm looking to connect with founders in your portfolio who might be thinking about acquisitions or strategic partnerships as a growth lever — especially in health/wellness and SaaS. The sweet spot is Series B-D companies that don't have a corp dev team yet.

Would any of your portfolio companies come to mind? Happy to share more context on what I'm doing if helpful.

Thanks,
Ilene
```

### Template B — Value-First
```
Hi [Name],

Quick note — I started Avera earlier this year after a decade running M&A at Zendesk and Wellhub (closed $1B+ in deals). I help growth-stage founders execute acquisitions without building a full internal team.

I've been seeing a lot of activity in [health/wellness / SaaS / their focus area] around smaller acquisitions — teams, products, market entry plays. If any of your portfolio founders are exploring that, I'd love to be a resource.

No hard ask — just wanted to be on your radar. Would love to catch up if you have 15 minutes.

Best,
Ilene
```

## Target List: Who to Reach Out To

### VCs to Contact
- Investors who led rounds for companies on your prospect list
- VCs in health/wellness and SaaS who regularly back Series B-D
- Anyone you've met through Wellhub (SoftBank network) or Zendesk

### Former Colleagues
- Zendesk corp dev team, leadership
- Wellhub exec team, board contacts
- BlackRock colleagues now at VCs or startups

### Founder Communities
- YC founder Slack/forums (if accessible)
- On Deck, Reforge, or other operator networks
- Industry-specific groups (digital health, SaaS)

## Follow-Up Cadence

| Timing | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Day 0 | Send initial message |
| Day 7 | Gentle follow-up if no response |
| Day 21 | Light touch — share a relevant article or LinkedIn post |
| Day 45 | Circle back with a specific ask or update |

---

## Key Principles
- Lead with the relationship, not the ask
- Make it easy for them: include a forwardable blurb (see outreach-templates.md)
- Don't ask everyone at once — stagger over 2-3 weeks so you can handle responses
- Track all outreach in prospect-tracker.md
