## Event Playbook

**Before:**
- Review prospect tracker for companies attending or speaking
- Prep 2-3 talking points (not a pitch — genuine curiosity about their growth plans)
- Have a one-liner ready: "I run an M&A advisory for growth-stage founders — previously led corp dev at Wellhub and Zendesk"

**During:**
- Lead with questions, not services
- Ask: "Are you thinking about acquisitions as a growth lever?" or "What's your biggest challenge scaling right now?"
- Collect LinkedIn connections, not business cards

**After:**
- Same-day LinkedIn connection request referencing the conversation
- Follow up within 48 hours with a short note
- Add to prospect tracker with "Met at [Event]" in Notes column
