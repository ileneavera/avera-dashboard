What I'm Watching

Mental health continues to be one of the most active sectors. Grow Therapy, Talkiatry ($210M Series D in February), and Headway ($2.3B valuation) are all scaling fast, and the sector is ripe for consolidation. Several of these companies are in my pipeline.

The healthcare AI space is also heating up. Hippocratic AI hit a $3.5B valuation, Tennr raised $101M for AI patient referrals, and Nabla is now in 85,000+ clinician workflows. These are the kinds of companies that will be both acquirers and acquisition targets in the next 12 to 18 months.
